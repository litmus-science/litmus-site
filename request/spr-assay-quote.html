What we need to send you an accurate quote

Most SPR quotes are scoped within a day when we have these details up front. If you're not sure about something, submit anyway — the lab will follow up before committing to a run.

  • ✓ Target protein identity, construct, and species
  • ✓ Analyte identity — small molecule, peptide, or protein
  • ✓ Whether purified protein is already in hand or needs sourcing
  • ✓ Expected affinity range, if known (nM, µM, etc.)
  • ✓ Buffer or solubility constraints on the analyte
  • ✓ Target timeline for results
